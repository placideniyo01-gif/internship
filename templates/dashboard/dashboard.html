INTERNSHIP SAVING PLATFORM

1. Available Balance

Your Available Balance represents the funds currently available in your wallet.

  • You can use it for eligible withdrawals.
  • You can use it to transfer funds to other users.
  • Available Balance does not earn the 5% daily interest.
  • When you block funds, the selected amount moves from Available Balance to Blocked Amount.

2. Blocked Amount

Blocking allows you to set aside funds for a fixed period of three calendar months.

  • Blocked funds earn interest at the platform's stated rate of 5% per day.
  • Blocked funds cannot be withdrawn or transferred while they are locked.
  • Your unlock date is set when you make your first block.
  • If you add more funds to an existing block, the original unlock date remains unchanged.
  • Newly added funds start earning interest from the time they are added.
Important: Only block funds that you are prepared to keep locked until the applicable unlock date.

3. How Daily Interest Works

The platform calculates interest on your eligible Blocked Amount, not on your Available Balance.

  • The stated interest rate is 5% per day.
  • Interest is calculated progressively based on the time funds remain blocked.
  • Interest is accumulated in your Interest Wallet.
  • When the block reaches its unlock date, interest stops accruing on that block.
Remember: The interest displayed on your dashboard relates to your blocked funds. It is not interest on your entire wallet balance.

4. What Happens When Funds Unlock?

Once the three-calendar-month blocking period reaches its unlock date:

  • Your blocked principal is released back into Available Balance.
  • The interest earned remains in your Interest Wallet.
  • You can then use the released principal according to the platform's available wallet functions.

Your account is updated when the platform processes your account activity or dashboard refresh.

5. Claim Interest

The Claim button allows you to move your accumulated interest from your Interest Wallet into your Available Balance.

  • Claimed interest becomes part of your Available Balance.
  • Available Balance does not automatically earn interest.
  • A seven-day waiting period applies between eligible interest claims.
  • You can view your current interest on the dashboard before claiming.

6. Deposits

You can add funds to your account through the Deposit section.

  • The minimum deposit is 25 USDT.
  • You must provide the required payment proof.
  • Your deposit remains Pending while it is awaiting review.
  • Once approved, the deposit is credited to your Available Balance.
  • Rejected deposits are not credited to your wallet.

7. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are made from your Available Balance only. Blocked funds and unclaimed interest cannot be withdrawn directly.

  • Withdrawal requests require the necessary wallet details.
  • Requests are subject to platform validation and administrator approval.
  • When fewer than two active referrals qualify, the minimum withdrawal amount is 50 USDT.
  • A waiting period is applied based on your most recent withdrawal request.
  • Your balance is processed according to the withdrawal approval process.

8. Internal Transfers

The Transfer feature allows you to send funds to another registered user on the platform.

  • Transfers use Available Balance only.
  • Blocked funds cannot be transferred.
  • Always verify the recipient's wallet details before confirming a transfer.
  • Completed transfers are recorded in your transaction history.

9. Referral Program

You can invite new users using your personal referral link. Referral rewards are tracked separately from your normal blocked-fund interest.

  • Share your referral link with people you invite.
  • Referral activity becomes eligible when the invited user makes a qualifying approved deposit.
  • The applicable referral rate depends on the qualifying deposit amount.
  • Your dashboard displays your referrals and referral reward information.
  • Use the referral Claim option to claim eligible referral rewards.

10. Transaction History

The History section allows you to review your wallet activity, including deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.

Always review your transaction records to keep track of your account activity.

11. Support

If you experience a problem or need assistance, use the Support button on your dashboard to contact the support team.

  • Explain your issue clearly.
  • Provide relevant transaction information when necessary.
  • Check your support notifications for replies.

12. Account Security

  • Keep your login details private.
  • Never share your password with another person.
  • Check wallet addresses carefully before making transfers or withdrawals.
  • Contact Support if you notice suspicious activity.
Final Notice: Please understand the difference between Available Balance, Blocked Amount, and Interest Wallet before making financial transactions. Always review the amount and details before confirming an action.
