👥 Referral Program

Every member receives a personal referral link. Share your referral link with friends, family members, and other investors. When someone registers using your referral link and makes their first deposit, you receive a % referral commission.

  • Level 1 Referral Commission(when referred user first deposit < 100 usdt): additional of 0.1% on daily interest
  • Level 2 Referral Commission (when referred user first deposit < 500 usdt): additional of 0.2% on daily interest
  • Level 3 Referral Commission (when referred user first deposit > 500 usdt): additional of 0.3% on daily interest

The standard account interest rate is 5% daily. Referral commissions are calculated separately according to the referral deposit amount and the applicable referral rate.

N.B: referral commission valid until end of 3months. The more active referrals you bring, the higher your earnings become.

📈 Daily Profit System

Interest starts accumulating only after your first deposit has been approved by the administrator. The platform calculates interest at a rate of 5% per day, calculated every second based on your current balance. When another deposit is approved, interest already earned is calculated first, and future interest continues using your updated balance. When you claim your interest, the claimed amount is added to your balance, and interest continues accumulating on the new balance.

Interest can be claimed once every 7 days.

💸 Withdrawals

  • Enter your correct wallet address carefully.
  • Admin reviews and processes withdrawals manually.
  • Approved withdrawals are sent directly to your wallet.
